01 / About

Language learning with people at the center.

I am an English lecturer and researcher at BINUS University, Indonesia. My academic work includes English language teaching, linguistics, communication, and the use of digital tools to support learning.

I am particularly interested in how learners experience technology: not only whether a tool works, but how it supports motivation, understanding, independence, and meaningful communication.

Publicly available professional biographies note that I began my higher-education career in 2010 and earned my undergraduate and master's degrees from Brawijaya University.

04 / Approach

Curiosity, clarity, and purposeful technology.

Teach for real communication.
Connect English learning with contexts learners can recognize and use.

Design for the learner.
Consider usability, feedback, and learner experience when working with digital tools.

Keep technology purposeful.
Use technology to support human learning, not as a substitute for it.
